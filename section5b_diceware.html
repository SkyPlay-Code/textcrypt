How Diceware works

Diceware generates passphrases by randomly selecting words from a large wordlist. Each word is chosen using crypto.getRandomValues, providing log₂(wordlistSize) bits of entropy per word. With the EFF Large Wordlist (7,776 words = 65 = 6 dice rolls), each word contributes ~12.9 bits. A 6-word passphrase gives ~77 bits of entropy — equivalent to a fully random ~13-character mixed-case alphanumeric password, but far more memorable. Passphrases are considered the gold standard for human-memorable secrets.
