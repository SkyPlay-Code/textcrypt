How entropy is calculated

Entropy = log₂(poolSize) × length bits, where poolSize is the total number of unique characters in your selected sets. Each character chosen at random from a pool of N contributes log₂(N) bits of uncertainty to an attacker. All characters are selected using crypto.getRandomValues with rejection sampling: bytes outside an even multiple of the pool size are discarded and resampled, eliminating modulo bias that would make some characters slightly more probable than others.
