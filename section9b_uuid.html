How UUID v4 is generated

UUID v4 (RFC 4122) is a 128-bit identifier where 122 bits are cryptographically random. The remaining bits encode the version (bits 12-15 of the third group set to 0100 = version 4) and the variant (top 2 bits of the fourth group set to 10 = RFC 4122 variant). Format: xxxxxxxx-xxxx-4xxx-yxxx-xxxxxxxxxxxx where y ∈ {8,9,a,b}. Using crypto.getRandomValues ensures the 122 random bits are cryptographically unpredictable. The collision probability for 1 trillion UUIDs is approximately 1 in 10²¹ — negligible.
