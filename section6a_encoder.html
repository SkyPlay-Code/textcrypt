About the encoding formats

Base64 — 64-char alphabet (A–Z a–z 0–9 +/), pads with =. Standard for email attachments, JWTs, and binary-in-JSON. Base32 — 32-char alphabet (A–Z 2–7), case-insensitive. Used in TOTP seeds, file systems that are case-insensitive. Base58 — Bitcoin's alphabet (no 0, O, I, l to avoid visual ambiguity). Used in crypto addresses, IPFS hashes. Hex — 16-char alphabet (0–9 a–f). Universal; used in hashes, cryptographic keys, color codes. Binary — raw 0/1 bit string. Space-separated bytes for readability. URL encoding — percent-encoding (%XX) for safe transmission in URLs.
