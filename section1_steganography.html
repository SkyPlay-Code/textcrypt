How it works

The payload is first encrypted using AES-256-GCM. The key is derived from your password and a random salt via PBKDF2. The resulting ciphertext, salt, and IV are converted to binary bits and embedded into the Least Significant Bits (LSB) of the RGB channels of the image, keeping the visual alteration mathematically imperceptible.

How it works

Translates your secret text into binary, then maps `0` and `1` to invisible zero-width Unicode characters (Zero Width Joiner and Zero Width Non-Joiner). These invisible characters are interleaved immediately following your chosen emoji. Because they have zero width, the text appears completely normal.

How it works

Functions identically to the Emoji tool but uses a different set of invisible characters (Word Joiner U+2060 and Invisible Times U+2062) to embed the binary data immediately after the first character of the cover text. Note: Pasting into some plain-text strict terminals may strip these characters.

How it works

Directly parses the RIFF/WAV binary headers to locate the raw PCM data chunk. It modifies the Least Significant Bit (LSB) of the audio samples to inject binary data. The subtle modification introduces negligible background noise, making it undetectable to the human ear.
