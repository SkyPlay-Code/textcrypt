XChaCha20-Poly1305

✔ 192-Bit Nonce
How it works · Why XChaCha20?

Standard ChaCha20 uses a 96-bit (12-byte) nonce. If random nonces are generated, the Birthday Paradox makes collisions possible after approximately 248 (or 232 in conservative multi-user models) messages.

XChaCha20 extends the nonce to 192 bits (24 bytes). This guarantees that randomly generated nonces will never collide across the operational lifetime of the key (safe for over 296 random encryptions), making it ideal for stateless operations, multi-file vaults, and cloud sync.
